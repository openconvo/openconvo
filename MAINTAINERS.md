# Maintainers

OpenConvo has a single maintainer, [@tompec](https://github.com/tompec), who
owns the roadmap, reviews every change and cuts every release.

## How decisions are made

- The maintainer decides, guided by the [roadmap](docs/roadmap.md) and its
  non-goals. There is no committee, vote or steering group.
- Issues are the way to influence those decisions: a well-argued bug report
  or feature request is read and answered. Code is not accepted from outside
  the maintainer team today; [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) explains why.
- Additional maintainers, if any, are invited individually and listed here.
  Adding one is the maintainer's decision.

## Contact

- Security: [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
