# Code of conduct

OpenConvo's issue tracker and every other project space exist to work on the
software. Everyone taking part is expected to be respectful and constructive:
argue about the code, not the person; assume good faith; no harassment,
discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing of other people's private
information.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments and issues that break this, and
may block repeat offenders from the project's GitHub spaces.

To report a problem, email [conduct@openconvo.com](mailto:conduct@openconvo.com).
Reports are handled by the maintainer, kept confidential, and acknowledged
within a few business days.
